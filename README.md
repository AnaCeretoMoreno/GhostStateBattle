## 🎮 Game Overview

**GhostStateBattle** is a competitive two-player game set in a near-future reality where housing prices have skyrocketed. In this world, the only way to secure a place to live is by *earning it*—through supernatural means.

Players take part in a haunted house **showing competition**, where the property in question is filled with ghosts. A real estate agent introduces the house to potential buyers, but in order to win ownership, they must prove themselves by eliminating as many ghosts as possible.

Only one player can walk away with the deed.

---

## 🕹️ Core Gameplay

* The match begins with a **house presentation scene**, where the first player to stand on the carpet is designated as *Player 1*.
* Once both players are ready, the **competition begins** inside the darkened house.
* Players are represented by dynamic light sources and must **use their light to eliminate ghosts**.
* The player who reaches **20 points first** (ghosts = 1 pt, special ghosts = 3 pts) **wins the house**.
* Ghosts appear and disappear randomly, and once one player captures a ghost, it **becomes unavailable to the other**.
* **Crossing through walls** will cause a player's light to flicker and temporarily disable their ability to kill ghosts.

---

## 🧠 Concept & Mood

This game humorously reflects today’s housing crisis by exaggerating the lengths people must go to for a place to live.
**GhostStateBattle** turns this struggle into a surreal, fast-paced competition where haunted homes are no longer just scary—they’re *the prize*.

Will you survive the supernatural and outscore your rival, or will you be left... *homeless*?
